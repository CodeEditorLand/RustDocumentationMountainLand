StatusBarProvider: creates and updates status bar items.

§StatusBarProvider (Environment)

Implements the StatusBarProvider trait for the MountainEnvironment. This provider handles creating, updating, and removing status bar items, and orchestrates communication between the Cocoon sidecar and the Sky frontend.
